- Fixed Cost: a cost that occurs at a constant level regardless of production volume
- Variable Cost: a cost that changes with production/sales volume
- Direct Cost: a cost that can be directly traced to a specific product or service
- Indirect Cost: a cost spread across multiple departments/products that is difficult to clearly attribute

## Step 4. Judgment Questions (KM044, KM045)

- Can a profit still be left after covering these activities and costs?
- Is the structure Fixed-Cost-heavy or Variable-Cost-heavy? What does that mean for the pressure to secure sales volume?
- Is the structure one in which per-unit profit remains sufficient as sales volume grows?
- If Manufacturing Cost (or purchase cost) is high, is there room to lower unit cost by cutting distribution stages or increasing production volume?
