## Content

In the bean-to-bar chocolate example, the revenue structure was designed around an online D2C channel to absorb the high manufacturing cost, and the core target was narrowed to premium vegan customers. The effective market size was calculated by combining the product price, the number of target customers, and annual consumption volume. Because the buyer is an individual consumer, "at what price will how many people buy how often" becomes the core logic explaining Qty.

The example calculates the annual effective market size using a base-unit price of KRW 19,000, a vegan and potential-vegetarian population of about 1.5 million people, and an annual consumption volume.

> This figure is not the most current market statistic as of today — it is the example value from when the manuscript was written, shown to illustrate what data the framework uses as Qty in a B2C context. Treat it as unverified/estimated.
