# CH04-EX03. Groundwater Level Measurement Equipment B2G Public Procurement Case

- Related Chapter: CH04. How Channel and Customer Relationship Reshape Price Structure
- Related MN/KM: MN06 / KM048
- What this case illustrates: In a public procurement channel, a price band is set between consumer price and actual selling price to reflect transaction structure such as discounts, commissions, and consignment sales fees, with direct proposal-based sales to local governments used as the primary channel.

## Content

In the doctoral dissertation's groundwater level measurement equipment case, a price band was set between consumer price and actual selling price. This was to account for discounts, commissions, consignment sales fees, and the possibility of future supply-price negotiations with distribution partners during the public procurement process. At the same time, direct proposal-based sales targeting local governments was set as the primary channel.

Source: Doctoral dissertation (2022), B2G case — price structure and channel structure.
