# CH04-EX02. Cosmetics Container B2B Bulk Order Case

- Related Chapter: CH04. How Channel and Customer Relationship Reshape Price Structure
- Related MN/KM: MN06 / KM048
- What this case illustrates: In a B2B transaction, bulk order volume reshapes unit price and margin structure, and direct proposal-based sales that cut out intermediate distribution steps lower fees.

## Content

In the cosmetics container B2B case, the official list price and the actual selling price under bulk-order terms differ. The structure was designed so that, once an order of around 200,000 units was secured, unit price could be lowered while still maintaining a margin of about 25%, with economies of scale from mass production as the key premise. The company reduced intermediate distribution steps and lowered fees by selling directly to cosmetics manufacturers through direct proposal-based sales.

Source: *Pricing Is Strategy*, pp. 208–215.
