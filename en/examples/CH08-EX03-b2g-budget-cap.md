# CH08-EX03. B2G Effective Market Calculation with a Budget Cap (Hypothetical)

- Related Chapter: CH08. From Qualitative to Quantitative — Effective Market Sizing and P×Qty
- Related MN/KM: MN07 / KM062

## Think it through

[Practice-only hypothetical data]. A company wants to sell a measuring instrument to public institutions. The equipment price is 5,000,000 KRW, and there are 1,200 institutions that could become installation targets. The total for the relevant budget line item that can purchase this equipment is 1.2 billion KRW.

1. What is the price multiplied by the number of potential installation targets?
2. What should be regarded as this business's effective market? Roughly how many units of equipment is that?
3. Besides the number of institutions, what should be checked when setting Qty?

- What this case illustrates: In B2G, it is the budget that can be executed, not price × number of potential installation targets, that caps the market — confirmed with numbers.

## Content

1. 5,000,000 KRW × 1,200 sites = 6,000,000,000 KRW, that is, 6 billion KRW.
2. Because the budget that can be executed is 1.2 billion KRW, the effective market is min(6 billion KRW, 1.2 billion KRW) = 1.2 billion KRW. In equipment, 1,200,000,000 ÷ 5,000,000 = 240 units, which is 20% of the 1,200 target institutions.
3. Check whether there is a legal obligation to install or manage the equipment, which budget line item it is executed from, how large that budget is, and whether the approach is direct sales or a partner channel. In the public market, institutions and budget are an additional constraint that defines market size.

> All numbers are assumptions meant to practice the structure and are not actual market facts.
