# CH10-EX03. Gross Profit and Contribution Margin of a 33,000 KRW Product

- Related Chapter: CH10. MODE A — Current Price Diagnosis
- Related MN/KM: MN06 / KM044~KM046

## Think it through

[Practice-only hypothetical data] A product sells at 33,000 KRW including VAT (VAT rate 10%). Direct cost is 9,800 KRW. Variable costs are a payment fee (2.8% of the gross payment) and parcel delivery of 3,200 KRW.

1. What are net sales excluding VAT?
2. What are Gross Profit and the Gross Profit margin?
3. What is the total variable cost, and on which amount is the payment fee calculated?
4. What are Contribution Margin and the Contribution Margin rate? What difference do you see compared with the Gross Profit margin?

<details>
<summary>Show answer and walkthrough</summary>

1. Net sales = 33,000 ÷ 1.10 = 30,000 KRW.
2. Gross Profit = 30,000 − 9,800 = 20,200 KRW. Gross Profit margin = 20,200 ÷ 30,000 = 67.3%.
3. The fee is based on the gross payment (33,000 KRW), so 33,000 × 2.8% = 924 KRW. Total variable cost = 924 + 3,200 = 4,124 KRW.
4. Contribution Margin = 20,200 − 4,124 = 16,076 KRW, and the Contribution Margin rate = 16,076 ÷ 30,000 = 53.6%. This is 13.7 percentage points lower than the Gross Profit margin of 67.3%, because selling and delivery variable costs were subtracted; Contribution Margin tells you how much is left from selling this product through this channel.

- What this case illustrates: Settle the input arrangement first (whether VAT is included, the fee basis), then obtain MODE A's two metrics in order. The same product is carried on in CH11-EX02 (target price back-calculation) and CH12-EX02 (allowable cost back-calculation).
