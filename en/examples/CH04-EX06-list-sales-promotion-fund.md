# CH04-EX06. The Promotion Fund Seen Through the Gap Between List Price and Sales Price

- Related Chapter: CH04. How Channel and Customer Relationship Reshape Price Structure
- Related MN/KM: MN06 / KM048

## Think it through

In the three cases of CH04-EX01 to EX03 (B2C chocolate, B2B cosmetics container, B2G groundwater level measurement equipment), there is a 20% band between the recommended consumer price (official price, List price) and the actual selling price (Sales price). Assume that all of the mass-produced volume is sold with no loss rate.

| Case | List price | Sales price |
|---|---|---|
| B2C | 19,000 KRW | 15,900 KRW |
| B2B | 1,500 KRW | 1,250 KRW |
| B2G | 1,320,000 KRW | 1,100,000 KRW |

1. What is the promotion cost per unit in B2C and B2B?
2. In B2G, with a monthly production volume of 500 units, what is the promotion budget available over the first year?
3. [Practice-only hypothetical data] If a product with a Sales price of 48,000 KRW has a List price of 120% of the Sales price and sells 300 units per month, what are the promotion fund per unit and the annual scale?
4. In the B2C case, how does margin change if this fund is not used at all? (Based on a total cost of 11,385 KRW and the diagram's CR cost of 3,180 KRW)

<details>
<summary>Show answer and walkthrough</summary>

1. Promotion cost per unit = List price − Sales price. B2C is 19,000 − 15,900 = 3,100 KRW, and B2B is 1,500 − 1,250 = 250 KRW.
2. 220,000 KRW per unit (1,320,000 − 1,100,000) × 500 units × 12 months = 1,320,000,000 KRW (1.32 billion KRW).
3. List price = 48,000 × 1.2 = 57,600 KRW, fund per unit = 9,600 KRW, annual = 9,600 × 300 × 12 = 34,560,000 KRW.
4. The margin in the diagram is 4,515 KRW (28.4% of the Sales price). If only half of the fund (1,590 KRW) is used, it becomes 6,105 KRW, and if none is used, it becomes 7,695 KRW (the latter two values are assumed calculations). This is why the customer-relationship fund must be set in advance within the price structure.

- What this case illustrates: A practice exercise calculating, across three transaction types, that the gap between List price and Sales price is the source of funds for promotion and customer-relationship management such as discounts and gifts. The B2C figure of 3,100 KRW (19,000 − 15,900) and the diagram's CR cost of 3,180 KRW (20% of the Sales price) differ by 80 KRW, which is read as the List price being a rounded value [Needs confirmation].
